## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. The Operator Login & Boot Sequence
When you first open WAKE Engine, you are greeted by an authentic terminal boot sequence. 
- You will be asked to enter your **Operator ID** (e.g., "JUSTIN" / "WAKE") to access the engine.
- You can configure the **System Voice** by clicking the top `Voice` control. The engine will speak to you using local desktop speech synthesis. You can choose from **Villain** (a deep, slow system voice), **Sentinel** (a firm command voice), or **Calm** (a softer assistant voice).

### 2. The Next-Step Rule
WAKE is built so you never stare at a blank screen. It is governed by the **Next-Step Rule**:
1. If you have no material, go to the **Console** and paste your source.
2. If you have source material, hit **Create Campaign** to run the agents.
3. If the campaign is built, export it to the **Library**.
4. If you want a secure receipt of your work, hit **Save Snapshot** in the Audit page.

## 🎛️ The 7 Core Abilities (How to use the buttons)

WAKE Engine is divided into 7 core "Ability Pages", accessible via the navigation tabs.

### 1. Console (The Workbench)
This is your starting point. 
- **Action:** Paste your raw source material (notes, transcripts, project briefs) into the text box.
- **Action:** Click **"Save Source"** to lock it in.
- **Action:** Click **"Create Campaign"** to trigger the Autopilot. The engine will read your source and instantly build multi-platform content (TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, X).

### 2. Agents (The Interpreters)
WAKE Engine comes with autonomous agents attached directly to it.
- **Action:** Click **"Run Tier Zero Agents"** to let the agents analyze your current source.
- **Chat:** Use the chat box to ask your agents direct questions (e.g., *"What is the strongest hook from my notes?"*). You can even use the **Speak** button to talk to them via microphone.
- **Action:** Click **"Promote Output"** to turn a great agent response into an official project export.

### 3. Cluster (The Campaign Network)
Once the agents are finished, this page holds your completed campaign.
- **Action:** Browse the generated **Platform Lanes** to see your scripts, hooks, captions, and visual image prompts.
- **Action:** Click **"Export Cluster"** to package all this content into a tidy export bundle.

### 4. Vault (Your Local Storage)
The IP Vault is how WAKE Engine securely scans your machine for inspiration.
- **Action:** Click **"Drive / Folder Intake"** to point the engine at a specific folder on your C: Drive or a USB drive.
- **Action:** Click **"Import Listed Folders"** and the engine will scan your documents and images, pulling them directly into the engine for the agents to use.

### 5. Library (The Memory)
WAKE Engine remembers everything it builds. 
- Use the **Library** to browse through all your past saved sources, generated outputs, and exported campaigns. Nothing is lost.

### 6. Monitor (The Machine Room)
For advanced operators.
- **Action:** Check this tab to view real-time runtime telemetry, view what the internal agents are currently processing, and verify the health of the system.

### 7. Audit (The Receipt Drawer)
Because WAKE Engine operates on a secure Write-Ahead Log (WAL), your work is extremely durable.
- **Action:** Click **"Save Snapshot"** to manually save a cryptographic local receipt of your current session. If the power ever goes out, WAKE Engine can rebuild your session exactly as you left it.

## 🛠️ Summary of the Workflow

To get the most out of WAKE Engine, simply follow this loop:
1. Go to **Vault** to scan a folder of notes, OR go to **Console** and type/paste your ideas.
2. Hit **Create Campaign**.
3. Go to **Cluster** to review the TikTok/LinkedIn/X content the agents built for you.
4. Hit **Export** to save your final assets.

> [!NOTE]
> **Fully Automated, Fully Yours:** WAKE Engine is entirely automated and designed to run completely offline if needed. It shifts the power of advanced AI creation directly to your local desktop.
